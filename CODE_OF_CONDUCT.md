# Code of conduct

TaBello follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good faith, give and take criticism about the
work and never about the person, and leave out harassment, insults and personal attacks of any kind.

If something happens that makes you or someone else feel unwelcome, tell the maintainer privately
from the repository's *Security* tab (**Report a vulnerability**, which reaches only the maintainer)
or with a message to [JackF007](https://github.com/JackF007) on GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially, and unacceptable behaviour can lead to a warning or a ban from the project.
