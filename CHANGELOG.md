# Changelog

## 0.1.0-beta.1 — first public beta

TaBello turns audio and video recordings into guitar, bass and ukulele tabs, on your computer.

- **Transcription**: notes detected by Spotify's Basic Pitch; tempo, key, time signature (with
  tempo changes and a metronome taken out), tuning and capo detected; melody or accompaniment;
  playing techniques (bends, slides, hammer-ons, pull-offs, vibrato); chord names and diagrams.
- **Isolate an instrument from a band** with Demucs, downloaded once (about 100 MB).
- **Watches the hand in videos** (MediaPipe): where the fretting hand is seen, notes are written where
  it plays, and the tab is checked against it.
- **Practice**: playback with real instrument sounds or along the original recording, the neck
  lighting each note as it plays, loop, slower speeds, count-in, metronome, transpose with a capo.
- **Edit by taking the notes**: drag them on the staff or the tab, choose their value, move them
  elsewhere on the neck; save versions of your edits and go back to any of them.
- **Export** to Guitar Pro 7, MIDI or alphaTex.
- Installers for Windows and Linux; macOS builds return once they can be signed.
